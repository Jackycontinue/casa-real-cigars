Our Heritage

For over a century, Casa Real has embodied the art of premium cigar making — from seed to smoke.

🌿

Finest Tobacco

We source exclusively from the world's most celebrated growing regions — Vuelta Abajo in Cuba, Cibao Valley in the Dominican Republic, and Estelí in Nicaragua. Each leaf is hand-selected for its exceptional quality and flavor profile.

🤲

Master Torcedores

Our cigars are crafted by master rollers, each with decades of experience. Every Casa Real cigar is shaped by hand, ensuring the perfect draw, even burn, and impeccable construction that defines luxury.

⏳

Patient Aging

Time is the most crucial ingredient. Our cigars rest in Spanish cedar-lined aging rooms for 12 to 60 months, allowing the tobaccos to marry and develop the refined complexity that connoisseurs treasure.

Craftsmanship

Every Casa Real cigar is the result of an unhurried, meticulous process passed down through generations.

🌱
Step 01 — Cultivation

Seed to Harvest

Our tobacco begins as carefully selected seeds planted in the richest soils of Cuba, Nicaragua, and the Dominican Republic. Each plant is tended by hand — from the first delicate seedling to the moment the priming leaves are harvested at peak maturity. Only the finest leaves earn the Casa Real name.

🍃
Step 02 — Fermentation

The Art of Fermentation

Harvested leaves enter a carefully controlled fermentation process lasting weeks to months. Natural heat and humidity break down harsh compounds while concentrating flavors. Our master fermenters turn and inspect each pilón daily, ensuring the leaves develop their full aromatic potential.

✋
Step 03 — Rolling & Aging

Hand-Rolled Perfection

Expert torcedores assemble each cigar using the traditional entubar method — bunching each filler leaf individually for a flawless draw. After rolling, cigars rest in Spanish cedar aging rooms where the tobaccos marry into a harmonious whole, emerging ready to deliver an extraordinary smoking experience.
